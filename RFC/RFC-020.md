# RFC-020 — Reliability, Support, dan Commercial Launch

## 1. Metadata dan urutan
- Status: **Deferred**.
- Horizon: tahap akhir roadmap komersial; bukan MVP akademik.
- Relevansi: launch governance untuk **F63**, **F64**, dan **F73 hanya bila disetujui terpisah**.
- **F65–F72 tetap di luar scope**.
- Prasyarat: **RFC-001 sampai RFC-019 seluruhnya selesai dan lulus**.
- Pendahulu langsung: **RFC-019 — Production Security dan Fraud Prevention**.
- Penerus langsung: **Commercial General Availability (GA)**.
- Commercial Entry Gate (CEG): **belum disetujui**.
- Eksekusi wajib sekuensial; GA menunggu Definition of Done RFC ini.
- Live credential dan real traffic hanya boleh aktif setelah setiap required gate PASS.

## 2. Ringkasan dan sasaran
RFC ini merancang SLO/SLA, high availability, capacity, disaster recovery,
on-call, support tooling/runbook, launch checklist, progressive rollout, rollback, dan kill switch.
Kontrak teknis siap diterapkan setelah keputusan bisnis, legal, finance, security, reliability, dan support tersedia.
RFC tidak menciptakan target SLO/SLA, RTO/RPO, volume, staffing, jam support, atau rollout percentage.
Sasaran:
1. Menetapkan SLI/SLO/SLA yang terukur, dimiliki, dan disetujui.
2. Membuktikan HA, capacity, graceful degradation, backup, serta DR.
3. Menyiapkan on-call, support queue, escalation, status page, dan runbook.
4. Menggabungkan evidence RFC-001–019 dalam launch gate default-deny.
5. Meluncurkan bertahap dengan cohort, hold, abort, rollback, dan kill switch.
6. Mengaktifkan live credential melalui dual control hanya setelah semua gate PASS.

## 3. Scope
- SLI/SLO/error budget, customer SLA/remedy, burn-rate alert, dan reporting.
- HA topology, scaling, quotas, capacity/soak/stress, backpressure, serta degraded mode.
- Backup, restore, failover/failback, disaster declaration, RTO/RPO, dan continuity.
- On-call schedule/escalation, support case/access, knowledge base, runbook, dan incident handoff.
- Immutable release, gate evidence, live-secret ceremony, launch stage, status page, dan GA approval.
- Progressive rollout, finance/fraud/support monitoring, rollback, freeze, serta granular kill switch.

## 4. Non-scope
- Menetapkan nilai komersial yang belum diputuskan atau menjanjikan SLA sebelum approval.
- Mengaktifkan uang nyata hanya karena RFC/test selesai.
- Menentukan F73 pricing/fee; bila tidak disetujui, monetisasi tetap nonaktif.
- Mengimplementasikan F65–F72.
- Memecah modular monolith menjadi microservices tanpa bukti kebutuhan.
- Mengasumsikan offline scanner, multi-region active-active, atau multi-currency.
- Mengubah kontrak payment/refund/security RFC-018–019.

## 5. Prasyarat lengkap
### Bisnis, legal, dan finance
- CEG final disetujui dengan launch owner dan stop authority.
- Merchant of record, KYC, terms/privacy, refund/dispute, tax, settlement, payout, dan liability final.
- F63/F64 selesai; F73 hanya jika memiliki approval terpisah.
- Target market, launch cohort, event eligibility, forecast, peak factor, dan loss budget disetujui.
- SLA/remedy, support channel/hour/language, serta escalation disetujui.
- UU PDP, DPA, subprocessor, retention/deletion, dan breach process disetujui.
### Engineering, security, dan operasi
- RFC-001–019 Done; regression evidence immutable dan belum expired.
- Defect blocker/critical dan security Critical/High terbuka nol.
- Pentest/retest, MFA/risk, incident drill, secret rotation, backup/restore lulus; topology, provider tier/quota, DB limit, scaling, dan cost guardrail dipilih.
- Observability, synthetic, alert routing, pager, status page, audit, production contract, provider escalation/monitoring/certification tersedia.
- Primary/secondary on-call, Incident, Finance Ops, Risk, Support, Legal, Privacy, war room, decision log, contact tree, dan rollback authority diuji.

## 6. Unresolved decision gates
- `DG-020-01`: SLI/SLO/window/error budget; SRE + Product.
- `DG-020-02`: SLA/exclusion/remedy; Legal + Business; `DG-020-03`: RTO/RPO/backup retention; Business + SRE + Legal.
- `DG-020-04`: forecast/peak/headroom/cost; Product + Finance + SRE.
- `DG-020-05`: HA topology/provider tier; Architecture + SRE.
- `DG-020-06`: on-call coverage/escalation; Operations + HR; `DG-020-07`: support channel/hour/response; Support + Product.
- `DG-020-08`: cohort/stage/hold/abort threshold; Launch owner + Risk.
- `DG-020-09`: credential custodian/approval; Security + Finance; `DG-020-10`: kill-switch authority/degraded mode; Incident commander.
- `DG-020-11`: GA criteria/post-launch review; Executive owner.
- `DG-020-12`: F73 enabled/disabled dan fee presentation; Product + Finance + Legal.
Semua gate default **BLOCKED**; placeholder dan nilai contoh dilarang pada production config/contract.

## 7. Arsitektur tekstual
```text
Clients → DNS/CDN/WAF → stateless Next.js instances pada failure domain disetujui
  ├─ application/readiness
  ├─ webhook handlers
  └─ protected jobs/workers
  → pooled PostgreSQL/Neon → external payment/identity/storage/email providers
Logs/metrics/traces/synthetic → SLO evaluator/alert router → on-call/status page
Backup/snapshot → isolated restore → invariant/smoke/reconciliation → failover procedure
Release artifact → gate evaluator → credential ARMED → staged cohort → GA
```
Topology final menunggu `DG-020-05`; multi-region/active-active tidak diasumsikan.

## 8. Aliran launch
1. Candidate dibangun sekali dengan digest, provenance, SBOM, dan migration checksum.
2. Pipeline mengumpulkan gate RFC-001–019, security, performance, DR, finance, dan support.
3. Gate gagal bila result bukan PASS, evidence expired, atau approval tidak lengkap.
4. Custodian memasang live credential ke secret manager; capability tetap `ARMED`, traffic nol.
5. Checker berbeda memverifikasi scope/key/endpoint tanpa membuka nilai secret.
6. Launch owner mengaktifkan cohort pertama sesuai `DG-020-08`.
7. SLO, reconciliation, fraud, support, security, dan provider dipantau selama hold.
8. Abort threshold memicu `HELD/ABORTED` dan `FROZEN/DISABLED`.
9. Kenaikan cohort memerlukan review dan approval baru.
10. GA hanya setelah `DG-020-11` dan seluruh exit criteria PASS.

## 9. Aliran incident dan support
```text
Customer/Synthetic/Alert → Support case atau alert → triage + identity verification
  → known issue/runbook atau escalation → incident declaration
  → contain/kill switch → communicate → recover → reconciliation
  → customer resolution → post-incident corrective action
```
Support dilarang memutasi payment/refund/payout/risk/ticket langsung di database.
Semua tindakan memakai API RFC pemilik, scoped authorization, MFA/step-up, dan audit.

## 10. SLI/SLO/SLA
- SLI wajib diputuskan untuk checkout/payment correctness/availability.
- Ukur webhook accepted-to-processed latency dan reconciliation lag.
- Ukur refund/dispute timeliness sesuai RFC-018.
- Ukur QR validation availability/latency dan check-in correctness.
- Ukur ledger/payout reconciliation freshness untuk F63.
- Ukur support first-response/resolution observasi per severity.
```text
availability = good_eligible_requests / total_eligible_requests
latency = eligible_requests_below_threshold / total_eligible_requests
correctness = correct_completed_operations / audited_completed_operations
error_budget = 1 - SLO_target
```
Eligibility, threshold, window, exclusion, low-traffic, dan burn rate berasal dari `DG-020-01`.
SLA tidak otomatis sama dengan SLO dan hanya berlaku setelah `DG-020-02`.

## 11. Skema database usulan eksak
Telemetry ber-volume tinggi tetap di observability provider; DB menyimpan governance/evidence metadata.

```sql
  query_ref VARCHAR(191) NOT NULL, status VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL, effective_at TIMESTAMPTZ,

  owner_user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id), related_type VARCHAR(32), related_id UUID, source_channel VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL,
  policy_version_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES commercial_policy_versions(id),
  opened_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, first_responded_at TIMESTAMPTZ, resolved_at TIMESTAMPTZ, closed_at TIMESTAMPTZ,
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL
);
```
```sql
CREATE TABLE release_candidates (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, release_id VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL UNIQUE, git_sha CHAR(40) NOT NULL,
  artifact_digest VARCHAR(191) NOT NULL UNIQUE, schema_version VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
  status VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL, created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, approved_at TIMESTAMPTZ, deployed_at TIMESTAMPTZ
);
CREATE TABLE launch_gate_results (

  evidence_sha256 CHAR(64) NOT NULL, expires_at TIMESTAMPTZ, checked_by UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id),
  checked_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, UNIQUE(release_candidate_id,gate_code)

CREATE TABLE launch_stages (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, release_candidate_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES release_candidates(id),
  sequence INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK(sequence>0), cohort_ref VARCHAR(191) NOT NULL,

CREATE TABLE production_capabilities (
  capability VARCHAR(64) PRIMARY KEY, state VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL, config_version VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
  changed_by UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id),
  approved_by UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id), changed_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,

```
```sql
CREATE TABLE dr_drills (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, drill_no VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL UNIQUE, scenario VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
  status VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL, source_recovery_point_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, started_at TIMESTAMPTZ,
  recovered_at TIMESTAMPTZ, measured_rpo_seconds BIGINT CHECK(measured_rpo_seconds>=0),
```

## 12. Kontrak API eksak
- Semua admin/ops endpoint: scoped role, MFA/step-up, RFC-014 envelope, no-store, audit.
- `GET /api/admin/operations/slo`; ops/SRE; query `service,window,cursor`;
  200 `{objectives:[{service,sli,target,window,observed,budgetRemaining,status}],asOf}`.
- `GET /api/admin/support/cases`; support; filter `status,severity,category,owner,cursor,limit<=100`;
  200 `{items,nextCursor}` dengan PII minimum.
- `POST /api/admin/support/cases/:id/actions`; support + step-up; `Idempotency-Key`,`If-Match`;
  body `{action,reasonCode,notes?,targetOwnerId?}`; financial action diteruskan ke RFC-018 API.
- `GET /api/admin/releases/:id/gates`; reviewer;
  200 `{releaseId,status,gates:[{code,result,evidenceHash,checkedAt,expiresAt}]}`.
- `POST /api/admin/releases/:id/approve`; approver + step-up;
  body `{scope,decision:"APPROVE"|"REJECT",reason}`; 409 jika required gate belum PASS.
- `POST /api/admin/launch/stages/:id/transition`; launch owner + checker;
  body `{to:"READY"|"ACTIVE"|"HELD"|"COMPLETED"|"ABORTED",reason}`.
- `POST /api/internal/operations/capabilities/:name/transition`; control-plane signed identity;
  body `{from,to,configVersion,changeRequestId,requestedBy,approvedBy}`.
- `GET /api/health/live|ready`; kontrak RFC-014; detail topology/secret tidak dibuka.
- Error: `SLO_CONFIG_INVALID`, `RELEASE_GATE_BLOCKED`, `EVIDENCE_STALE`,
  `LAUNCH_STATE_CONFLICT`, `DUAL_CONTROL_REQUIRED`, `CAPABILITY_FROZEN`, `DR_GATE_FAILED`.

## 13. State
- Release: `BUILT → VERIFYING → BLOCKED|APPROVED → DEPLOYED → ROLLED_BACK`.
- Stage: `PLANNED → READY → ACTIVE → HELD|COMPLETED|ABORTED`.
- Capability: `DISABLED → ARMED → ENABLED → FROZEN → DISABLED`.
- Support: `OPEN → TRIAGED → WAITING_CUSTOMER|ESCALATED → RESOLVED → CLOSED`.
- DR: `PLANNED → RUNNING → PASSED|FAILED`.
`ARMED` berarti secret tervalidasi tanpa traffic; `FROZEN` menolak mutasi baru namun menjaga read/reconciliation.
Transisi memakai optimistic version/transaction dan audit append-only.

## 14. HA, capacity, dan performance
- Runtime stateless direplikasi pada failure domain yang disetujui.
- DB pooling, max concurrency, autoscaling bound, provider quota, dan cost guardrail diuji.
- Test mencakup forecast, peak, webhook/scan burst, refund/dispute, payout, dan support.
- Headroom/saturation berasal dari `DG-020-04`.
- Backpressure, queue age, retry budget, circuit breaker, dan load shedding diuji per use case.
- Payment/ledger/refund/dispute/payout/check-in tidak boleh fail-open.
- Email/reporting boleh degraded hanya bila policy menyetujui; UI jujur; cache publik bukan source of truth transaksi.

## 15. Backup, DR, on-call, dan support
- Frequency/retention/RTO/RPO hanya dari `DG-020-03`.
- Restore isolasi memverifikasi schema, checksum, invariant, ledger, dan smoke.
- Skenario: corruption, region/provider outage, secret compromise, bad deploy/migration, queue backlog.
- Failover/failback menjaga webhook idempotency dan mencegah split-brain writer.
- Alert harus actionable, owned, severity-tagged, dashboarded, dan memiliki runbook.
- Support tooling menerapkan least privilege, masking, access audit, dan session timeout.
- Verification tidak meminta password, OTP, recovery code, atau full payment data.
- Runbook minimum: rollback, DB restore/failover, webhook backlog, provider outage,
  invariant/ticket/QR, account takeover, fraud, refund/chargeback, payout, secret, privacy, status page.
- Runbook berisi trigger, precondition, safe action, validation, escalation, rollback, dan owner.

## 16. Security, compliance, error, dan observability
- Live secret hanya di production secret manager dengan dual control dan access log.
- Production access JIT/least privilege; change freeze dan exception process berlaku.
- Audit/retention/legal hold/DSR/breach/PCI/provider compliance wajib PASS.
- F73, bila aktif, tampil transparan dan mengikuti refund/ledger allocation; F65–F72 tetap tidak tersedia.
- Error code: `SLO_*`, `CAPACITY_*`, `RELEASE_GATE_*`, `LAUNCH_*`, `SUPPORT_*`, `DR_*`.
- Log: release, digest, stage, capability, change request, correlation, outcome; tanpa secret/PII.
- Metrik: SLI/saturation/queue/DB/provider/webhook/ledger/fraud/refund/QR/support.
- Alert threshold, paging, escalation, dan status-page trigger hanya dari decision gate.
- Synthetic transaction memakai akun/metode disetujui dan selalu direkonsiliasi.

## 17. Strategi pengujian
- Unit/integration: gate evaluator, state, maker-checker, support authorization.
- Load/soak/stress: forecast/peak yang disetujui, saturation, serta recovery.
- Failure injection: instance, DB pool, queue, provider, dan observability.
- DR: restore, failover/failback, reconciliation, measured RTO/RPO.
- Release: bad artifact, stale evidence, migration/readiness failure, rollback.
- Support/on-call: routing, verification, escalation, masking, page/ack/handoff/status.
- Finance: payment/refund/dispute/payout/F73 fee bila aktif selama stage.
- Security: secret ceremony, revoke, incident, vulnerability regression.
- E2E: allowlist checkout hingga check-in/support; certification sebelum live.

## 18. Launch checklist dan progressive rollout
### Sebelum credential
- RFC-001–019 Done; CEG dan `DG-020-01..12` ditutup; seluruh evidence fresh.
- Legal/privacy/tax/merchant/provider/F63/F64/F73 bila relevan PASS.
- Security/pentest/IR, capacity/HA/DR, support/on-call, observability, rollback PASS.
### Sebelum traffic pertama
- Digest/migration/config/DNS/TLS/backup/readiness/synthetic/reconciliation PASS.
- Secret dipasang dual-control; capability hanya `ARMED`.
- Cohort, hold, abort, owner, war room, status page, dan kill switch siap.
- Setelah approval checker, capability dapat `ENABLED`.
### Tahap
1. `0% / ARMED`: validation tanpa traffic.
2. Internal synthetic/certification.
3. Cohort allowlist sesuai `DG-020-08`.
4. Kenaikan hanya setelah hold dan multidisiplin review.
5. Abort input: SLO burn, fraud, reconciliation, provider, support, security.
6. Abort mengubah stage `ABORTED` dan capability `FROZEN/DISABLED`.
7. GA hanya setelah semua stage dan `DG-020-11` PASS.

## 19. Rollback dan kill switch
- Kill switch: registration, organizer onboarding, publish, checkout, payment,
  webhook mutation, refund, payout, dispute, dan QR check-in.
- Freeze menolak write berisiko namun menjaga status/read/support/reconciliation aman.
- Rollback ke artifact digest sebelumnya; migration backward-compatible.
- Data/ledger dikoreksi dengan compensating transaction, bukan delete/down migration.
- Credential direvoke/rotate dual-control; failover mengikuti runbook.
- Setelah rollback wajib smoke, invariant, ledger, backlog, impact, dan status update.

## 20. Kriteria penerimaan
1. SLI/SLO/SLA memiliki owner, formula, window, dashboard, alert, dan approval.
2. Capacity/HA/degradation memenuhi forecast/headroom yang disetujui.
3. DR memenuhi RTO/RPO tanpa melanggar invariant finance/ticket.
4. On-call, support, escalation, status page, dan runbook lulus drill.
5. Gate default-deny menolak failure, stale evidence, dan approval tidak lengkap.
6. Credential hanya `DISABLED → ARMED → ENABLED` dengan dual control.
7. Rollout/abort/rollback/freeze/kill switch lulus.
8. F63/F64 sesuai kontrak; F73 terpisah; F65–F72 tidak diimplementasikan.
9. GA tidak terjadi sebelum setiap required gate PASS.

## 21. Risiko
- SLA tanpa kapasitas: pisahkan SLO/SLA dan block sebelum gate.
- Test tidak mewakili peak: owned forecast, soak/burst, production-like limit.
- Split brain: single-writer invariant dan controlled failover.
- Alert fatigue: actionable routing, noise review, staffing gate; direct support mutation: scoped API, no DB write, MFA, audit.
- Rollout cepat: immutable stage, hold, abort, checker; secret prematur: ARMED, dual control, default disabled.
- Rollback/schema/provider gagal: expand/backfill/contract, prior-artifact drill, idempotent inbox/outbox, degraded UI, dan reconciliation.

## 22. Definition of Ready
1. RFC-001–019 Done dan evidence fresh.
2. CEG dan `DG-020-01..12` ditutup.
3. SLO/SLA, capacity/topology, RTO/RPO, staffing/support, rollout, dan GA charter final.
4. Provider production contract dan isolated production-like environment tersedia.
5. Schema/API/state direview Engineering, SRE, Security, Product, Finance,
   Legal, Privacy, Risk, Support, dan executive launch owner.
6. Runbook/dashboard/alert/synthetic/drill/cohort/war room/rollback siap.
7. Live credential belum aktif dan capability `DISABLED`.
8. F73 diputus terpisah bila relevan; F65–F72 tidak dimasukkan.

## 23. Definition of Done
1. Acceptance, regression, security, performance, resilience, DR, dan support test lulus.
2. SLO/SLA monitoring, HA/capacity, backup/DR, on-call/support/status page operasional.
3. Candidate, gate evidence, migration, secret ceremony, dan dual approval tervalidasi.
4. Progressive rollout menyelesaikan tiap stage tanpa bypass.
5. Finance reconciliation, fraud/security, support, dan provider health memenuhi GA charter.
6. Rollback/freeze/failover/revoke/kill switch terbukti; blocker/Critical/High/break = 0.
7. Dokumentasi tanpa TODO, secret, data nyata pada fixture, atau target rekaan.
8. Executive owner menyetujui GA hanya setelah semua gate PASS.
9. Jika gate gagal, launch berhenti/rollback; status Deferred berlaku sampai CEG resmi mengubahnya.
