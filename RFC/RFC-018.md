# RFC-018 — Refund, Chargeback, dan Dispute

## 1. Metadata dan urutan
- Status: **Deferred**.
- Horizon: roadmap komersial, bukan MVP akademik dan bukan otorisasi implementasi.
- Fitur utama: **F64 — Refund Finansial Produksi**.
- Dependensi fitur: **F63** menyediakan uang nyata, ledger, settlement, dan payout.
- **F73** hanya relevan bila monetisasi disetujui terpisah; **F65–F72 tetap di luar scope**.
- Prasyarat: **RFC-001 sampai RFC-017 seluruhnya selesai dan lulus**.
- Pendahulu langsung: **RFC-017**.
- Penerus langsung: **RFC-019 — Production Security dan Fraud Prevention**.
- Commercial Entry Gate (CEG): **belum disetujui**.
- Eksekusi wajib sekuensial; selama CEG belum disetujui hanya pengisian keputusan yang diizinkan.
- RFC ini tidak mengizinkan uang nyata, credential produksi, traffic produksi, atau migrasi produksi.

## 2. Ringkasan dan sasaran
RFC ini merancang refund penuh/parsial, chargeback, dispute, evidence, dan alokasi koreksi ke ledger/payout.
PostgreSQL tetap source of truth; provider berada di balik adapter; setiap efek eksternal idempoten dan dapat direkonsiliasi.
Kebijakan eligibility, biaya, SLA, liability, serta hasil dispute tidak ditentukan di sini.
Sasaran:
1. Mencegah total refund melebihi nilai payment yang telah captured.
2. Menangani full/partial refund dan retry provider secara deterministik.
3. Merekam chargeback/dispute/evidence secara immutable dan terotorisasi.
4. Mengalokasikan reversal ke ledger/payout tanpa mengubah histori.
5. Menyediakan queue operasional, audit, SLA terkonfigurasi, dan recovery.

## 3. Scope
- Request, review, approval, eksekusi, inquiry, dan rekonsiliasi refund produksi.
- Refund per order item atau nominal; full/partial ditentukan policy yang disetujui.
- Ingestion chargeback/dispute, deadline, evidence, representment/acceptance, dan outcome.
- Financial adjustment terhadap ledger, settlement, payout hold/reversal dari F63.
- Provider adapter, webhook inbox, outbox worker, support/admin API, audit, metric, dan alert.
- Policy/config version agar setiap keputusan dapat direproduksi.

## 4. Non-scope
- Menentukan merchant of record, hak konsumen, eligibility, fee, pajak, liability, atau SLA.
- Menentukan perlakuan tiket Used/Unused; keputusan wajib masuk gate.
- Mengaktifkan provider/refund produksi atau memindahkan uang.
- Mengimplementasikan monetisasi F73; hanya extension point allocation bila disetujui.
- Mengimplementasikan F65–F72, resale, promo, kursi, dynamic pricing, atau multi-mata uang.
- Menjamin kemenangan dispute atau menyamarkan fungsi provider yang tidak tersedia.

## 5. Prasyarat lengkap
### Produk, legal, dan finance
- CEG disetujui tertulis dengan owner dan tanggal berlaku.
- Merchant of record dan pihak kontraktual pembeli ditetapkan.
- Policy refund per reason, event/ticket state, kanal, dan periode disetujui.
- Policy chargeback, representment, acceptance, write-off, appeal, dan liability disetujui.
- Fee, pajak, reserve, saldo negatif, pembulatan, dan chart of accounts disetujui.
- Dasar pemrosesan evidence, UU PDP, retensi, legal hold, serta penghapusan disetujui.
- Terms, privacy notice, dan komunikasi pelanggan direview Legal.
### Teknis dan provider
- RFC-001–017 beserta regression, audit, backup/restore, dan security gate lulus.
- Implementasi F63 menyediakan double-entry ledger, rekonsiliasi, settlement, dan payout.
- Provider produksi, API version, metode, refund/dispute/evidence capability, webhook, dan report dipilih.
- Dukungan partial refund dan aturan rounding tiap metode telah dicertification-test.
- Object storage privat, malware scan, queue/scheduler, secret manager, log, metric, dan alert tersedia.
- Privileged identity, maker-checker, approval matrix, serta runbook outage/mismatch tersedia.

## 6. Unresolved decision gates
- `DG-018-01`: eligibility refund; owner Product + Legal; bukti policy version.
- `DG-018-02`: full/partial, minimum, maksimum, rounding; Finance + Product.
- `DG-018-03`: fee/pajak/provider cost refundable; Finance + Tax + Legal.
- `DG-018-04`: liability chargeback, reserve, saldo negatif; Finance + Legal.
- `DG-018-05`: SLA internal/provider dan escalation; Support + Provider Ops.
- `DG-018-06`: evidence field, format, size, retention, legal hold; Legal + Security.
- `DG-018-07`: auto-submit, maker-checker, dan approval; Risk + Operations.
- `DG-018-08`: provider/capability per metode; Payment owner.
- `DG-018-09`: dampak refund pada Ticket/Order; Product + Legal.
- `DG-018-10`: F73 aktif atau tetap nonaktif; Product + Finance.
Semua gate default **BLOCKED**. Production config menolak placeholder, fallback, dan nilai kebijakan implisit.

## 7. Arsitektur tekstual
```text
Buyer/Admin/Support → Refund/Dispute API → Auth + Policy Resolver
  → Application Service → PostgreSQL transaction
      ├─ refund cases/attempts
      ├─ disputes/events/evidence/submissions
      ├─ financial adjustments + F63 ledger/payout
      └─ audit + outbox
  → Outbox Worker → ProductionPaymentGateway adapter → Provider
Provider webhook/report → verify/deduplicate → canonical transition
  → ledger/payout allocation → notification/reconciliation
```

## 8. Aliran data
### Refund
1. Aktor mengirim idempotency key, alasan, item/nominal, dan policy version.
2. Service memverifikasi role, ownership, payment, ticket state, serta policy.
3. Transaksi mengunci payment lalu seluruh refund terkait dalam urutan tetap.
4. Hitung `refundable = captured - completedRefund - reservedRefund`.
5. Tolak nominal `<=0` atau `>refundable`; buat case, reservation, audit, dan outbox.
6. Worker memanggil provider dengan idempotency key stabil; respons dicatat sebagai attempt.
7. Hanya webhook/report tepercaya menyelesaikan case.
8. Completion membuat balanced ledger adjustment dan payout exposure sesuai policy.
9. Ticket/Order/notifikasi berubah hanya menurut `DG-018-09`.
### Chargeback/dispute
1. Verifikasi raw-body signature; deduplikasi external event ID.
2. Buat/perbarui dispute canonical dan exposure finansial.
3. Simpan deadline dalam UTC serta timestamp asli provider.
4. Evidence privat diunggah, divalidasi, dipindai, dan diaudit.
5. Maker menyusun submission; checker menyetujui bila diwajibkan.
6. Adapter mengirim evidence dan menyimpan receipt/hash.
7. Outcome provider membuat adjustment baru; histori tidak ditulis ulang.
8. Mismatch masuk reconciliation queue dengan owner dan severity.

## 9. Skema database usulan eksak
Konvensi: UUID/CUID non-predictable, uang `BIGINT` Rupiah, waktu `TIMESTAMPTZ`, tanpa hard-delete.
```sql
CREATE TYPE refund_kind AS ENUM ('FULL','PARTIAL');
CREATE TYPE refund_status AS ENUM
  ('REQUESTED','REVIEW_REQUIRED','APPROVED','REJECTED','PROCESSING','COMPLETED','FAILED','CANCELLED');
CREATE TABLE refund_cases (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, order_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES orders(id),
  payment_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES payments(id), requester_user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id),
  policy_version_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES commercial_policy_versions(id),
  kind refund_kind NOT NULL, reason_code VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, reason_detail TEXT,
  requested_amount_idr BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (requested_amount_idr > 0),
  status refund_status NOT NULL, provider_refund_id VARCHAR(191), provider_status VARCHAR(64),
  version INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 1 CHECK (version > 0), requested_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,
  decided_at TIMESTAMPTZ, completed_at TIMESTAMPTZ, created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,
  updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, UNIQUE(payment_id, provider_refund_id)
);
CREATE TABLE refund_case_items (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, refund_case_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES refund_cases(id),
  order_item_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES order_items(id), quantity INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK(quantity>0),
  amount_idr BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK(amount_idr>=0), UNIQUE(refund_case_id,order_item_id)
);
CREATE TABLE refund_attempts (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, refund_case_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES refund_cases(id),
  attempt_no INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK(attempt_no>0), idempotency_key VARCHAR(191) NOT NULL UNIQUE,
  request_hash CHAR(64) NOT NULL, outcome VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL, provider_request_id VARCHAR(191),
  error_code VARCHAR(64), attempted_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, UNIQUE(refund_case_id,attempt_no)
);
```
```sql
CREATE TYPE dispute_status AS ENUM
  ('OPEN','EVIDENCE_REQUIRED','UNDER_REVIEW','WON','LOST','ACCEPTED','CLOSED');
CREATE TABLE disputes (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, payment_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES payments(id),
  provider_dispute_id VARCHAR(191) NOT NULL UNIQUE, reason_code VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
  amount_idr BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK(amount_idr>0), status dispute_status NOT NULL,
  response_due_at TIMESTAMPTZ, provider_created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,
  version INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 1, closed_at TIMESTAMPTZ,
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL
);
CREATE TABLE dispute_events (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, dispute_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES disputes(id),
  provider_event_id VARCHAR(191) NOT NULL UNIQUE, event_type VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
  payload_hash CHAR(64) NOT NULL, occurred_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, received_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL
);
CREATE TABLE dispute_evidence (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, dispute_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES disputes(id),
  evidence_type VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, object_key VARCHAR(512) NOT NULL UNIQUE,
  content_sha256 CHAR(64) NOT NULL, mime_type VARCHAR(127) NOT NULL,
  size_bytes BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK(size_bytes>0), scan_status VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL,
  uploaded_by_user_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id), retention_until TIMESTAMPTZ,
  legal_hold BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE, created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL
);
CREATE TABLE dispute_submissions (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, dispute_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES disputes(id), version INTEGER NOT NULL,
  status VARCHAR(24) NOT NULL, prepared_by_user_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id),
  approved_by_user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id), provider_receipt VARCHAR(191),
  submitted_at TIMESTAMPTZ, UNIQUE(dispute_id,version)
);
CREATE TABLE financial_adjustments (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY, source_type VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL, source_id UUID NOT NULL,
  ledger_transaction_id UUID NOT NULL UNIQUE, payout_id UUID,
  amount_idr BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK(amount_idr<>0),
  allocation_version_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES commercial_policy_versions(id),
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, UNIQUE(source_type,source_id,allocation_version_id)
);
```
Indeks wajib: `(payment_id,status)` dan `(status,updated_at)` pada refund; `(status,response_due_at)`
dan `(payment_id)` pada dispute; `(dispute_id,occurred_at)` pada event; `(payout_id)` pada adjustment.
Total refund lintas-row dijaga row lock + transaction retry, bukan `CHECK`.

interface ProductionPaymentDisputeGateway {

## 11. Kontrak API eksak
- Semua endpoint: Zod, envelope RFC-014, `X-Correlation-Id`, `private,no-store`, audit.
- `POST /api/orders/:orderId/refunds`; buyer pemilik atau privileged user; `Idempotency-Key` wajib;
  body `{kind,reasonCode,reasonDetail?,amountIdr?,items?,policyVersion}`; 202 `{refund:{id,status,requestedAmountIdr,requestedAt}}`.
- `GET /api/refunds/:id`; pemilik/privileged; 200 `{refund:{id,orderId,kind,reasonCode,requestedAmountIdr,status,timestamps}}`.
- `POST /api/admin/refunds/:id/decision`; privileged + MFA; `If-Match`; body
  `{decision:"APPROVE"|"REJECT"|"CANCEL",reasonCode,notes?,policyVersion}`; 200/202 `{refund:{id,status,version}}`.
- `GET /api/admin/disputes`; scoped operator; filter `status,dueBefore,paymentId,cursor,limit<=100`;
  200 `{items:[{id,paymentId,reasonCode,amountIdr,status,responseDueAt}],nextCursor}`.
- `POST /api/admin/disputes/:id/evidence/presign`; scoped + MFA; body
  `{evidenceType,fileName,mimeType,sizeBytes,sha256}`; 201 `{evidenceId,uploadUrl,expiresAt,requiredHeaders}`.
- `POST /api/admin/disputes/:id/submissions`; maker + idempotency; body
  `{evidenceIds,statement,policyVersion}`; 201 `{submission:{id,version,status}}`.
- `POST /api/admin/disputes/:id/submissions/:sid/approve`; checker berbeda + MFA;
  body `{decision:"APPROVE"|"REJECT",reason}`; 202 jika mengantrekan provider.
- `POST /api/webhooks/payment-production`; provider signature; 202 valid/duplicate, 401 invalid.
- Error: `REFUND_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `REFUND_AMOUNT_INVALID`, `REFUND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`,
  `POLICY_VERSION_STALE`, `TICKET_STATE_CONFLICT`, `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`,
  `REFUND_STATE_CONFLICT`, `EVIDENCE_NOT_READY`, `SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`.

## 12. State dan konsistensi
```text
Refund: REQUESTED → REVIEW_REQUIRED → APPROVED → PROCESSING → COMPLETED
        REQUESTED|REVIEW_REQUIRED → REJECTED|CANCELLED
        PROCESSING → FAILED → PROCESSING (hanya retry aman)
Dispute: OPEN → EVIDENCE_REQUIRED → UNDER_REVIEW → WON|LOST|CLOSED
         OPEN|EVIDENCE_REQUIRED → ACCEPTED → CLOSED
```
Terminal state tidak dibuka kembali; reversal provider menjadi event/adjustment baru.
Mutasi memakai optimistic version dan lock berurutan `payment → refund → ledger/payout`.

## 13. Keamanan dan compliance
- Privileged action membutuhkan MFA/session freshness setelah RFC-019 lulus.
- RBAC, object scope, least privilege, maker-checker, dan audit append-only wajib.
- Evidence privat, encrypted, malware-scanned; presigned URL singkat dan akses tercatat.
- Webhook memakai signature, timestamp tolerance provider, uniqueness, dan key rotation.
- PII/evidence dilarang pada log, analytics, trace, screenshot, serta fixture.
- Retensi/legal hold hanya dari gate; DPA, subprocessor, data residency, breach process, dan UU PDP wajib PASS.
- PAN/CVV dan credential payment tidak disimpan.

## 14. Error, logging, SLA, dan performa
- Code stabil: `REFUND_*`, `DISPUTE_*`, `EVIDENCE_*`, `LEDGER_*`, `PAYOUT_*`, `PROVIDER_*`.
- Log aman: correlation, case, operation, state, policy version, outcome, duration; tanpa free-text/evidence/payload.
- Metrik: completion/failure, amount, open/due dispute, evidence failure, chargeback exposure, reconciliation break.
- SLA/alert threshold berasal dari `DG-018-05`; sebelum itu hanya synthetic alert.
- Provider call melalui outbox, bukan di transaksi DB; worker bounded, backoff+jitter, dead-letter review.
- Target latency/throughput/batch/backlog ditetapkan RFC-020; daftar selalu cursor-paginated.
- Exactly-once tidak diasumsikan; semua efek dibuat idempoten.

## 15. Strategi pengujian
- Unit/property: eligibility adapter, allocation, rounding config, state, dan batas total refund.
- Integration PostgreSQL: concurrent partial refund, lock/version, ledger posting, payout exposure.
- Contract provider: full/partial, duplicate, timeout, unknown, dispute, evidence, capability missing.
- Webhook: invalid, replay, out-of-order, unknown payment, duplicate outcome, key rotation.
- Security/privacy: IDOR, maker-checker bypass, malware/MIME spoof, URL expiry, redaction, retention.
- E2E certification: request → approval → provider → webhook → ledger → notification.
- Recovery: worker crash setelah provider menerima tetapi sebelum local commit.
- Reconciliation: report mismatch menghasilkan case dan compensating adjustment.
- Performance memakai volume/SLA yang telah diputuskan, bukan target rekaan.

## 16. Rollout, rollback, dan kill switch
1. Tutup CEG dan `DG-018-01..10`; bekukan policy/config version.
2. Terapkan schema additive, fake adapter, dan test; production capability tetap disabled.
3. Backfill identifier tervalidasi; rekonsiliasi opening balance.
4. Jalankan shadow webhook tanpa mutasi; lalu certification dengan maker-checker.
5. Pilot allowlist hanya setelah RFC-019 dan RFC-020 lulus.
6. Naikkan cohort hanya bila finance, SLA, security, dan support tetap PASS.
- Kill switch: `refund_request`, `refund_execution`, `dispute_submission`, `webhook_mutation`.
- Disable worker tidak menghapus case/inbox; ledger dipulihkan dengan compensating entry.
- Rollback memakai artifact sebelumnya dan schema backward-compatible; tanpa down migration destruktif.

## 17. Kriteria penerimaan
1. F64 full/partial refund mengikuti policy version yang disetujui tanpa policy rekaan.
2. Completed + reserved refund tidak pernah melebihi captured amount.
3. Chargeback/dispute/evidence idempoten, aman, dan dapat direkonsiliasi.
4. Setiap dampak memiliki balanced ledger transaction dan payout allocation terlacak.
5. F73 tetap nonaktif tanpa keputusan terpisah; F65–F72 tidak diimplementasikan.
6. Provider capability memiliki contract evidence atau blocking/manual runbook eksplisit.
7. Security, privacy, SLA, audit, rollout, rollback, dan kill switch lulus.
8. Tidak ada credential atau traffic produksi yang diaktifkan.

## 18. Risiko
- Policy ambigu: block tanpa policy version valid.
- Concurrent over-refund: lock, reservation amount, idempotency, invariant test.
- Provider menerima tetapi respons hilang: inquiry/reconciliation sebelum retry.
- Event out-of-order: immutable inbox dan canonical resolver.
- Evidence bocor: private storage, scan, access audit, retention gate.
- Ledger/payout berbeda: balanced constraint, reconciliation, compensating entry.
- Deadline dispute terlewat: due queue/alert berdasarkan SLA yang disetujui.
- Scope creep: exclusion F65–F72 dan gate F73.

## 19. Definition of Ready
1. RFC-001–017 Done dan regression evidence tersedia.
2. CEG dan `DG-018-01..10` APPROVED atau NOT_APPLICABLE beralasan.
3. F63 ledger/settlement/payout final dan opening balance terverifikasi.
4. Provider contract, certification account, webhook spec, dan fixture tersedia.
5. Schema/API/state/security direview Engineering, Finance, Legal, Risk, Support, dan Privacy.
6. Policy, approval matrix, SLA, evidence standard, retention, capacity, rollback, dan owner disetujui.
7. F65–F72 tidak diminta; F73 memiliki keputusan terpisah bila relevan.

## 20. Definition of Done
1. Semua acceptance, regression, provider contract, security, privacy, recovery, dan performance test lulus.
2. Schema, adapter, API, worker, audit, notification, reconciliation, serta runbook selesai.
3. Refund/chargeback/dispute selalu menghasilkan ledger/payout allocation balanced dan auditable.
4. Provider certification serta rollback/kill-switch drill lulus; Critical/High terbuka nol.
5. Dokumentasi policy/config sinkron, tanpa TODO, secret, PII, atau nilai rekaan.
6. RFC-019 dapat dimulai sekuensial.
7. Status Done tidak dengan sendirinya mengizinkan traffic atau credential produksi.
