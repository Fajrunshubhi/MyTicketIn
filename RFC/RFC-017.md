# RFC-017 — Settlement, Payout, dan Reconciliation

## Metadata dan urutan

- Status: **Deferred**.
- Klasifikasi: roadmap komersial, bukan MVP; **F63/F73** tetap `Won't Have`.
- Prasyarat: **RFC-016 selesai dan lulus** beserta seluruh prasyarat transitif RFC-001–015.
- Pendahulu langsung: **RFC-016 — Production Payment dan Financial Ledger**.
- Penerus langsung: **RFC-018**; scope dan judul RFC-018 belum ditetapkan oleh dokumen ini.
- Implementasi tetap mengikuti urutan ketat dan tidak boleh berjalan paralel dengan RFC-016.
- Rollout synthetic/sandbox/dark-mode; **live payout dilarang sampai launch gate komersial disetujui**.

## Kedudukan

RFC ini mendefinisikan fondasi teknis untuk menghitung kewajiban kepada organizer, membentuk batch settlement, mengirim payout melalui adapter, dan merekonsiliasi hasil.
Dokumen ini tidak menentukan hak ekonomi, jadwal pembayaran, reserve, withholding, pajak, atau beneficiary yang sah.
Keputusan bisnis/legal/finance wajib disediakan sebelum implementasi; kelulusan RFC-017 tidak berarti launch atau izin memindahkan uang.

## Sasaran

1. Menyediakan onboarding serta lifecycle beneficiary/rekening penerima secara aman.
2. Menghitung payable dari ledger immutable RFC-016 dan policy version yang disetujui.
3. Membentuk batch reproducible/auditable tanpa pembayaran ganda.
4. Mengisolasi provider melalui port canonical, webhook inbox, dan reconciliation.
5. Mendukung hold/reserve/adjustment serta command idempoten dengan live payout fail-closed.

## Ruang lingkup

- Beneficiary profile, payout account token/reference, verification, dan approval.
- Payable policy, eligibility, cutoff, calculation snapshot, serta payable item.
- Hold/reserve lifecycle dan release/forfeit/adjustment.
- Settlement period/batch/item, payout instruction/attempt, dan provider webhook.
- Reconciliation source/run/match/mismatch/exception/resolution.
- Finance console, maker-checker, audit, observability, synthetic adapter, test, runbook, dan kill switch.

## Non-scope

- Menentukan beneficiary legal, ownership rekening, jadwal/minimum payout, fee, tax, reserve, atau hold.
- Live payout/transfer/credential production; live charge tetap mengikuti gate RFC-020.
- Stored value, wallet saldo, escrow, lending, advance, atau netting.
- Cross-border/multi-currency/FX/crypto/cash serta refund/dispute/debt policy dan nasihat legal/akuntansi.
- Perubahan MoSCoW MVP atau aktivasi F63/F73.

## Unresolved decision gates

1. Beneficiary legal yang berhak, hubungan organizer/legal entity, dan ownership rekening.
2. Provider/bank/rail/protocol, SLA, limit, biaya, kalender, serta maintenance window.
3. Data rekening, hosted/tokenized onboarding, verification, masking, approval perubahan, cooling-off, step-up, dan notifikasi.
4. Settlement basis, cutoff/timezone/hari kerja, frequency, batas payout, dan carry-forward.
5. Formula gross/fee/tax/withholding/refund/dispute/adjustment/rounding serta negative payable/netting.
6. Reserve dan hold: type/rate/cap/duration/scope/review/appeal/release serta approver.
7. Failed/rejected/returned policy, retry, remediation, fee, dan beneficiary notification.
8. Reconciliation source/format/channel/checksum/tolerance, manual match, maker-checker, serta period close.
9. Provider idempotency, grouping, partial result, out-of-order event, dan sanctions/AML/fraud owner.
10. Launch/RTO/RPO gate: approver, evidence, limit, allowlist, rollout, rollback, on-call, SLA, dan komunikasi.

Setiap gate harus memiliki decision record, effective date, owner, dan approver legal/finance/compliance yang relevan.

## Prinsip arsitektur

- Ledger RFC-016 adalah source of truth; payable bukan balance yang dapat diedit.
- Snapshot immutable mengacu ke posting/policy; satu payable item maksimum satu batch aktif/final.
- Adapter tidak menentukan domain; instruction dan attempt dipisah agar retry tidak menggandakan transfer.
- Outcome `UNKNOWN` dibekukan untuk reconciliation, bukan dikirim ulang otomatis.
- Hold/reserve auditable dan terkait posting ledger yang disetujui.
- Nominal memakai integer minor unit dan satu currency per batch.
- Live execution wajib memiliki launch artifact tervalidasi; ketiadaannya berarti deny.

## Arsitektur komponen tekstual

```text
RFC-016 Ledger + Payment Settlement Events
  → Payable Calculation Service
  → eligibility/cutoff/hold/reserve policy
  → Payable Items + Calculation Snapshot

Finance Scheduler/Admin
  → Settlement Batch Service → maker-checker
  → Payout Instruction → PayoutProvider port
  → Sandbox/Synthetic adapter only

Provider webhook/report
  → verified inbox/object import → Reconciliation Engine
  → exact match / candidate / mismatch / exception
  → ledger adjustment/reversal command (approved)

Organizer
  → Beneficiary/Payout Account UI
  → hosted provider/tokenized reference
```

## Aliran data tekstual

1. Hosted onboarding menghasilkan token/reference masked; perubahan rekening membuat versi dan cooling-off.
2. Pada cutoff, service membaca eligible postings dan policy version efektif.
3. Service menghitung komponen payable; snapshot menyimpan source IDs/hash agar reproducible.
4. Payable eligible dikunci ke batch berdasarkan entity/provider/currency/period.
5. Maker menyiapkan dan checker menyetujui; dark-mode hanya menghasilkan instruction synthetic.
6. Event/report idempoten direkonsiliasi berdasarkan reference, amount, currency, dan status.
7. Mismatch masuk exception queue; resolusi finansial merujuk journal RFC-016 sebelum batch final.

## State machine **PROPOSED**

```text
PayoutAccount: DRAFT → PENDING_VERIFICATION → VERIFIED → ACTIVE | SUSPENDED | REJECTED | CLOSED
PayableItem: CALCULATED → HELD | ELIGIBLE → ALLOCATED → SETTLED | CANCELLED
SettlementBatch: DRAFT → PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED → PROCESSING → PARTIALLY_COMPLETED | COMPLETED | FAILED | CANCELLED
PayoutInstruction: CREATED → SUBMITTED → PROCESSING → SUCCEEDED | FAILED | RETURNED | UNKNOWN | CANCELLED
ReconciliationRun: CREATED → INGESTING → MATCHING → REVIEW_REQUIRED → COMPLETED | FAILED
```

State, transisi, terminal behavior, dan reopening rule tetap **PROPOSED**.

### Invariant database **PROPOSED**

1. Account `ACTIVE` harus verified dan melewati cooling-off.
2. `net_minor = gross - fee - tax - refund - dispute - hold - reserve + approved_adjustment`.
3. Source posting tidak dihitung dua kali dan payable item maksimum satu batch aktif/final.
4. Total batch sama dengan item; currency konsisten.
5. Idempotency key maksimum satu provider reference; `UNKNOWN` wajib direkonsiliasi sebelum retry.
6. Batch `COMPLETED` hanya jika semua item terminal sesuai policy.
7. Hold/reserve memakai journal RFC-016; row finansial tidak di-hard-delete.

## Payable calculation **PROPOSED**

- Input hanya committed ledger sequence dan settlement fact; calculation memakai cutoff, policy, entity, dan currency.
- Source posting IDs/hash disimpan agar reproducible; komponen gross/deduction harus eksplisit.
- Rounding mengikuti keputusan dan golden fixtures.
- Recalculation membuat superseding snapshot/journal, tidak mengubah sejarah.
- Cancellation/refund/dispute/negative/carry-forward mengikuti policy yang belum diputuskan.

## Hold dan reserve

- Hold scoped sesuai keputusan dan memuat reason, decision reference, owner, review, serta audit.
- Reserve berasal dari policy version sebagai payable component dan posting ledger.
- Release/forfeit membuat event/journal, bukan mengedit amount historis.
- UI membedakan `held`, `reserved`, `eligible`, `paid`; kriteria fraud/legal tidak diciptakan di sini.

## Reconciliation

- Ingestion memverifikasi source/signature/checksum/schema/period/currency/duplicate.
- Exact matching memakai reference+amount+currency; candidate tidak auto-resolve tanpa threshold.
- Missing/mismatch/duplicate/returned/unknown masuk exception queue.
- Resolution menyimpan before/after, evidence, maker/checker, serta linked journal.
- Run deterministic; artifact terbatas dan period close menunggu threshold/sign-off.

## State management dan caching

- PostgreSQL/ledger authority; provider report evidence eksternal; UI tidak optimistic untuk mutasi finansial.
- Page `private,no-store`; filter/pagination memakai URL.
- Batch wizard memakai server draft dan expected version, bukan global client state.
- Projection boleh private-cache singkat; submit memvalidasi ulang source sequence.
- Status provider hanya berubah melalui verified inbox/import.

## Keamanan

- Capability dipisah untuk account, read, prepare, approve, submit, reconcile, dan hold.
- Maker/checker berbeda sesuai gate; perubahan rekening memakai step-up dan out-of-band notification.
- Token/reference dienkripsi; UI/log masked; production secret absent dan live egress blocked.
- Webhook/import memakai signature/checksum, replay/size/content controls.
- Artifact encrypted/time-bound/access-logged sesuai RFC-015; kill switch menghentikan submit/retry.

## Kepatuhan

- KYC/KYB, privasi, retensi, DSR, dan vendor mengikuti RFC-015; MoR/ledger/fee/tax mengikuti RFC-016.
- AML/sanctions/fraud, banking, withholding, invoice, dan reserve legality memerlukan owner/legal advice.
- RFC tidak menyatakan dana escrow/aman/tersedia/terjamin; evidence mengikuti retensi final.

## Error, logging, dan observability

- Timeout menjadi `UNKNOWN` dan wajib lookup/reconcile; rejected/returned tidak mengubah account/payable.
- Duplicate/out-of-order idempoten; log pseudonymous tanpa rekening/nama/payload.
- Metrik mencakup batch/approval/submit/unknown/return/mismatch/lag.
- Alert dan runbook mencakup account risk, duplicate, unknown, mismatch, stuck batch, artifact, serta unauthorized action.

## Kinerja dan reliability

- Target **PROPOSED**: calculation/reconciliation 100.000 row ≤ 10 menit; finance read p95 ≤ 2 detik.
- Worker memakai chunk/checkpoint/`SKIP LOCKED`/backpressure; uniqueness tetap efektif multi-worker.
- Checksum dan restore drill wajib.
- SLA/RTO/RPO produksi tetap decision gate.

## Strategi pengujian

- Unit/property: eligibility, formula, hold/reserve, transisi, matching, no-double-allocation, dan totals.
- Integration: concurrency, allocation, approval, submit idempotency, immutable history.
- Contract/E2E: sandbox/fake → account → payable → batch → result → reconciliation.
- Security: isolation, maker-checker, step-up, masking, tamper, secret/data canary.
- Recovery/negative: unknown/partial/return/crash/duplicate/restore/kill switch; live submit selalu gagal.

## Rollout dan launch control

1. Tetap `Deferred` sampai RFC-016 dan seluruh gate RFC-017 selesai.
2. Migration additive di branch terisolasi; beneficiary/account hanya fake/hosted sandbox.
3. Jalankan payable shadow/golden test serta batch synthetic ke fake/sandbox.
4. Uji reconciliation, hold/reserve, restore, incident, dan rollback.
5. Deploy dark/read-only: `LIVE_PAYOUT_ENABLED=false`, secret absent, egress denied.
6. Launch gate menetapkan allowlist/limit/monitoring/owner/kill switch; kelulusan RFC bukan aktivasi.
7. Rollback mematikan command/worker tanpa menghapus sejarah finansial.

## Kriteria penerimaan

1. Keputusan beneficiary/payable/cutoff/reserve/hold/provider/reconciliation/launch terdokumentasi.
2. Account versioning, verification, masking, cooling-off, dan approval lulus.
3. Payable deterministic/reproducible, tanpa source atau allocation ganda; batch total konsisten.
4. Instruction/attempt/webhook idempoten termasuk `UNKNOWN`.
5. Reconciliation dan hold/reserve terkait ledger menghasilkan exception/evidence tanpa mengubah sejarah.
6. Security/isolation/audit/restore/kill switch lulus tanpa Critical/High.
7. Live payout disabled dan F63/F73 tetap roadmap tanpa perubahan MoSCoW.

## Risiko dan mitigasi

- Payout ganda/unknown: idempotency, uniqueness, freeze, reconcile sebelum retry.
- Account takeover/data leak: step-up, cooling-off, notification, hosted token, encryption, masking.
- Salah payable/batch: ledger-only, versioned policy, source hash, golden test, resumable worker.
- Salah reconciliation: exact-first, tolerance gate, review, immutable evidence.
- Penyalahgunaan hold/reserve: scoped capability, reason/review/appeal/audit.
- Aktivasi dini atau asumsi RFC-018: no secret/egress, launch validator; successor hanya nomor.

## Definition of Ready

1. RFC-016 selesai dengan ledger, fee/tax snapshot, dan dark evidence.
2. Seluruh decision gate material ditutup dan disetujui legal/finance/compliance/Product Owner.
3. Provider contract/sandbox/credential plan/due diligence tersedia.
4. API/schema **PROPOSED** difinalisasi dan migration plan direview.
5. Synthetic/golden data, environment, capacity, owner, threat/incident/recovery plan tersedia.
6. Launch gate didefinisikan tetapi belum aktif.

## Definition of Done

1. Kriteria penerimaan, decision record, migration, module, console, dan runbook selesai.
2. Semua test proporsional lulus dengan evidence checksum/allocation/idempotency/reconciliation/restore.
3. Tidak ada blocker/critical atau Critical/High keamanan/finance/compliance.
4. Deployment synthetic/sandbox/dark; secret absent, live egress blocked, payout disabled.
5. Artifact bebas secret/rekening mentah/PII; RFC-017 disetujui sebelum RFC-018.
6. Aktivasi payout tetap menunggu launch gate terpisah.
